This document is a facilitator-ready script and findings template. Complete it only after at least five moderated sessions have been conducted with informed consent. Do not enter identifying information or record participants without explicit consent.

## Research Questions

- Can a participant create a six-month vesting stream without help?
- Does the participant understand what the cliff means and when tokens become claimable?
- Can a recipient find and complete the claim flow?
- Which terms, steps, or decisions cause hesitation or errors?

## Moderator Script

### Introduction

Thank the participant, read the consent statement, and confirm that they may stop at any time. Explain that the prototype may contain nonfunctional or simulated actions. Ask the participant to think aloud. Do not explain the cliff or lead them toward a particular answer.

### Task 1: Create a six-month stream

> Imagine you sponsor a contributor with a six-month token grant. Create a stream for the recipient shown in the prototype. Choose a schedule that you think is appropriate, then stop on the review step before submitting.

Observe whether they can locate each step, understand the rate and duration fields, and explain the summary. Do not specify a cliff duration unless they ask; record their questions and assumptions.

### Task 2: Explain the cliff

> Looking at the schedule you created, explain in your own words what the cliff means. When do you think the recipient can first claim tokens, and what happens after that point?

Ask only neutral probes: “What makes you say that?” and “What would you expect to happen next?” Record the participant’s exact phrasing where practical.

### Task 3: Claim as recipient

> You are now the recipient of this stream. Find the stream and show how you would claim any available tokens. Tell me what you expect to happen before and after the cliff.

Observe whether they can identify the recipient view, claimable amount, claim action, and any wallet confirmation. Do not ask them to sign a real transaction.

### Wrap-up

> What felt unclear or harder than expected? What would you change first? Is there anything else you expected to see?

Thank the participant. Remove any temporary account or test data created for the session.
